Party Guide

How the Age Game Works

Everyone adds their age once, then makes one guess for the total age in the room. The closest total wins when the host hits Touch Down.

Guest Steps

1

Open My Age

Go to the guest link and stay on the My Age tab.

2

Enter Your Name and Age

Type your first name, enter your age, and press Save age.

3

Switch to Sum of Ages

Use the same first name you already entered. Each person gets one guess.

4

Guess the Total

Enter your best guess for the combined age of everyone in the room, then press Save guess.

Host Steps

1

Open the Dashboard

Use the host link on your phone or laptop and sign in with the admin password.

2

Watch Live Totals

You will see player count, guesses, and the real total age privately on the dashboard.

3

Close Guessing

Press Disable Guess Tab when everyone is done so no more guesses can be added.

4

Reveal the Winner

Press Touch Down to show the real closest guess. Use Mask Winner or Hide Winner if needed.

Quick Notes

The game accepts each first name only once in My Age and once in Sum of Ages. If a guest cannot connect, make sure they are on the same Wi-Fi and use the exact guest link shown above.

What To Tell People

  1. Open the guest link.
  2. Enter your first name and your age.
  3. Switch to the guessing tab.
  4. Use the same name and enter your guess for the total age in the room.
